# ADR-005: Human Approval Gates for Consequential Actions

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-10-05

## Decision

Application submission, sensitive disclosure, destructive operations, and
official evaluation require a persisted explicit approval record before action.

## Rationale

The student remains the authority for consequential decisions. Agents may
prepare drafts and request approval but cannot auto-approve or bypass a missing,
rejected, or expired approval.
